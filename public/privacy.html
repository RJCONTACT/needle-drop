Privacy & terms

What this site is

Needle Drop is a fan-made record player. Search results and music come from YouTube through the YouTube API Services. It is not made by or affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Terms

By using Needle Drop you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Privacy

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. Needle Drop doesn't collect or sell personal data.
  • What you search is sent to YouTube to get results. Searches are cached for a few hours on Cloudflare so repeat searches are faster; they are not linked to you.
  • "Hot this week" counts plays anonymously. When you play a record, Needle Drop stores only the song's YouTube ID and a one-way code made from your IP address and the date. Your IP address is never stored, the code changes every day, and counts older than a week are deleted.
  • The list of records you've played is saved only in your own browser (local storage). Clear your browser data to remove it.
  • The video player is YouTube's own, so YouTube may set cookies and collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
